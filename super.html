Real Problems in Uganda We Solve

❌ Problem 1: No Supercars for Hire

In Uganda, you can find Toyota Harrier but not a Mustang, Porsche, G-Wagon for rent. People use fake decorations. Events lose class.

❌ Problem 2: Fraud & Trust

Current car hire is WhatsApp based. Client sends money, car doesn't come. No contract, no insurance, no tracking.

❌ Problem 3: Owners Fear Theft

People who own luxury cars in UG fear to rent them out - no GPS, no driver verification, no damage guarantee.

❌ Problem 4: Tourists Want Experience

Muzungu tourists in Entebbe want to drive a luxury car through Murchison Falls, not a small Passo. No platform exists.

THE SOLUTION

What Our System Does

✅ Verified Booking + Contract

Auto-generates rental agreement PDF with National ID verification. No more fraud.

✅ Live GPS + Driver

Every car has GPS + optional professional driver. Owner tracks car live from phone.

✅ Mobile Money Payment

Pay 30% deposit via MTN MoMo / Airtel Money to lock car. Balance on delivery. Built for UG.

✅ Damage Insurance System

System calculates refundable damage deposit and checks car condition photos before/after.
